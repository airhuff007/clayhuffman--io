About

Clay Huffman

For years, I thought success meant pushing through stress and pain. Then a nerve injury sidelined me for four years and forced me to find a different approach.

That's when I discovered fascia release and mobility work. Simple movements that reduce tension, improve movement, and help your body function the way it's supposed to. They changed how I sleep, move, and manage stress — and now I help others do the same.

I also create music with my wife Lauren, including meditation playlists, while studying audio engineering at SAE Institute in Miami.

Beyond health and music, I build AI systems and business automations that help agents, artists, and small businesses eliminate bottlenecks, save time, and grow more efficiently.

Whether you're looking to improve your body, your business, or both — I'd love to connect.
