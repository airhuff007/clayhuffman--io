Clay's Let Me Put You On

Things worth trying, watching, doing, and thinking about. Starting from the simple stuff and working our way up to the more intense.

Movies That Built Me

The ones I watched over and over as a kid. Pure vibes, no analysis necessary.

The Sandlot

Summer. Baseball. Friendship. The Beast. This movie is perfect.

Intensity: Easy

Three Ninjas

Kids kicking ass and learning discipline. Rocky, Colt, and Tum Tum made martial arts look cool.

Intensity: Easy

Small Habits That Hit Different

Little things that make life better for no reason other than they just do.

Windows Down, Heat Blasting

When it's cold outside, roll the windows down and blast the heat. Fresh freezing air on your face, warm air on your body. It's chaos and it's perfect.

Intensity: Easy

Morning Sun, No Phone

Stand outside for 5 minutes in the morning. Just light. No scrolling. Watch how your energy changes for the rest of the day.

Intensity: Easy

Cold Water on Your Face

Splash cold water on your face when you're tired or foggy. Instant reset. Works better than coffee sometimes.

Intensity: Easy

Books That Rewired Me

Not just good reads. These books changed how I think about work, systems, and life.

Atomic Habits — James Clear

The best book on behavior change. Systems over goals. Identity over outcomes. Small changes compound.

Intensity: Medium

Deep Work — Cal Newport

How to do focused work in a distracted world. Changed how I structure my days and protect my attention.

Intensity: Medium

The War of Art — Steven Pressfield

Short. Brutal. About resistance and why we avoid the work that matters. Read it in one sitting.

Intensity: Medium

Physical Practices (Getting Intense Now)

These require commitment. But if you do them consistently, they change everything about how your body and mind operate.

Breathwork Sessions

Controlled breathing to regulate your nervous system. Calms you down or wakes you up, depending on the protocol. I make custom breathwork tracks — check them out on the homepage.

Intensity: Medium-High

Cold Exposure

Cold showers, cold plunges, ice baths. Uncomfortable as hell. Builds mental resilience and reduces inflammation. Start with 30 seconds of cold at the end of a shower.

Intensity: High

Fasting (16–24 Hours)

Skip breakfast. Or skip breakfast and lunch. Let your body rest from digesting. Mental clarity goes through the roof after hour 12. Not for everyone, but worth trying once.

Intensity: High

Ways of Thinking

Mental frameworks that help me make better decisions and waste less energy.

Inversion Thinking

Instead of asking "How do I succeed?", ask "How would I guarantee failure?" Then avoid those things. Easier to spot what breaks a system than what perfects it.

Intensity: Medium

Second-Order Thinking

Don't just ask "What happens if I do this?" Ask "And then what?" Look two or three steps ahead. Most people stop at the first consequence.

Intensity: Medium

Systems Over Goals

Goals are outcomes. Systems are repeatable processes. You don't rise to your goals — you fall to the level of your systems. Build the system, the results follow.

Intensity: Medium

Music for Focus

What I listen to when I need to lock in and do deep work.

Binaural Beats (Alpha/Theta Waves)

Specifically tuned frequencies that help you focus or relax. I use these during deep work sessions. Not placebo — the brain responds to frequency.

Intensity: Easy

Classical / Film Scores

Hans Zimmer, Ludovico Einaudi, Ólafur Arnalds. Cinematic and emotional without lyrics to distract you. Perfect for writing and building.

Intensity: Easy

Lo-Fi / Ambient

Chill beats, no vocals, just rhythm. Keeps your mind engaged without pulling focus. Great for coding, designing, or organizing.

Intensity: Easy
